## 1. Privacy Policy Summary
- **Data Collected:** Customer Name, Phone Number, License Plate, Service Status (*Queued*, *Washing*, *Ready*).
- **Purpose:** Sending automated transactional WhatsApp/SMS notifications as vehicle wash status changes.
- **Third-Party Subprocessors:** Meta Platforms, Inc. (WhatsApp Cloud API) and Supabase Inc. (secured with Row Level Security & JWT).
- **Data Deletion:** Unconditional right to request permanent deletion within 48 hours via [semihcakir04@gmail.com](mailto:semihcakir04@gmail.com).

## 2. Terms of Service Summary
- **Service Scope:** Operational car wash POS, queue tracking, and automated customer updates.
- **Messaging Terms:** Messages are transaction-based notifications; customers may opt out at any time upon vehicle drop-off or via email.
- **Liability:** Standard industry liability limits regarding pre-existing vehicle defects and carrier network latency.
